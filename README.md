# 🔒 Sub Vault: Subscription Tracker & Manager

**Sub Vault** is a simple, secure, and intuitive application designed to help you take control of your expenses, monitor recurring costs, and never get caught off guard by a surprise renewal again.

## 🚀 Features

* **Quick Add:** Just press "Add Subscription" and type in your information to start tracking instantly.
* **Centralized Dashboard:** View your total recurring spending at a glance.
* **Frictionless Tracking:** Clean, minimalist design with no unnecessary steps.
* **Privacy First:** Your data belongs to you—secure, organized, and completely under your control.
